The Song Game

How to play

  • Create 2 teams and set their names.
  • One player reads the English translation aloud.
  • Both teams can guess the Bollywood song.
  • Correct by singer team: +10 points.
  • Correct by opponent team: +5 points.
  • Next round starts with the other team.
